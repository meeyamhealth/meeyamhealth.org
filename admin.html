Donations

Donors fill the form at meeyamhealth.org/donate.html after paying. Check each one against the Federal Bank statement, then issue the receipt.

  1. Pending: find the amount and reference in the bank statement, then click Payment received.
  2. Verified: click Issue receipt. It gets the next receipt number.
  3. Receipted: click Email receipt to send the donor their link from Gmail.

The annual statement lists receipted 80G donations for the chosen financial year in the column order of the Income Tax donation statement (Form 10BD). It's due by 31 May after the year ends; ask your CA to check the columns against the current form before filing.
