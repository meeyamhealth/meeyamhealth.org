Questions

How much tax do I save?

Donations to Meeyam Health Foundation qualify for deduction under Section 80G (provisional approval, URN AAVCM5647HF20261), within the limits set by law, if you file under the old tax regime. The new tax regime doesn't allow this deduction. Your tax adviser can tell you the exact saving.

When will I get my receipt?

We email it within about 7 working days, after the payment reaches our bank. After the financial year ends, we report your donation to the Income Tax Department, and you can download the official donation certificate from your income tax login.

Why do you need my PAN and address?

The Income Tax Department requires us to report each donor's name, address and ID (PAN or Aadhaar) so your deduction can be verified. We use them only for this and never share them with anyone else.

I made a mistake in the form

Email meeyamhealth@gmail.com with your reference number and the correction.

Can I donate from outside India?

Not yet. We can only accept donations from Indian sources at present.
